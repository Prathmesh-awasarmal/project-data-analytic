# 🚀 Overview

This **Power BI Dashboard** analyzes **Spotify streaming performance** across **artists, genres, albums, countries, record labels, and social media platforms**, helping uncover business insights through interactive visualizations.

---

# 📊 Dashboard Highlights

| 🎧 Streams | 📅 Monthly | ⭐ Popularity | 🎤 Artists | 🌍 Markets | 🎵 Tracks |
|:---------:|:----------:|:------------:|:----------:|:----------:|:---------:|
| **526B** | **5B** | **51.09** | **499M** | **20** | **200+** |

---

# ✨ Features

🎯 Real-Time KPIs • 🌎 Geographic Heat Maps • 🎤 Artist Rankings • 🏷 Label Analysis • 🎵 Genre Insights • 🌍 Country Analysis • 📱 Social Media Metrics • 😊 Mood Analytics • ⚡ Interactive Filters

---

# 📈 Dashboard Modules

| 📊 Overview | 🎤 Artists | 🌍 Geography | 📱 Social |
|-------------|------------|--------------|-----------|
| KPI Cards | Top Artists | World Map | TikTok |
| Genres | Labels | Top Countries | Instagram |
| Languages | Revenue | Regions | Twitter |
| Albums | Market Share | Monthly Streams | Reddit |

---

# 📌 Key Insights

✅ Soul generated the highest stream volume

✅ Japanese became the top streamed language

✅ Germany, France & UK dominate Europe

✅ India is one of the fastest-growing markets

✅ Instagram Reels leads social engagement

✅ Romantic & Melancholic tracks receive higher interaction
